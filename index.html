Visualization method

The weekly heatmap shows string activity from 08:00 to 20:00. The five rows represent Strings 1–5, from top to bottom. Each square sums triggers over 2–20 minutes; the final interval includes only its remaining duration. Intervals adapt to the screen width between 2 and 20 minutes. The chart is centered when two-minute intervals fit, and scrolls horizontally when twenty-minute intervals do not. Hover over a square to see its date, string, time range and trigger count.

Square color and area share a quartile mapping. Nonzero intervals from all valid dates between 08:00 and 20:00 are pooled, with one trigger, Q1, the median, Q3 and the maximum as anchors. Squares grow smoothly as colors shift from green through yellow to red. The same scale is used across weeks at the same interval length, and recalculated when that interval changes. Tiny faint squares indicate zero triggers; missing logs are marked separately.

The daily view shows activity from 08:00 to 20:00 in two-minute intervals. The five rows represent Strings 1–5, from top to bottom, on a shared vertical scale. Hover over a curve to see its time range and trigger count.

Curve colors use a shared quartile scale from green through yellow to red, calculated from nonzero two-minute intervals across all valid dates. This scale stays consistent when dates change. The date picker uses a separate quartile scale based on full-day totals. Counts represent logged triggers.

The monthly heatmap groups dates by month, with six rows from Tuesday to Sunday. Mondays are omitted. Each square represents the full-day total across all five strings, from 00:00 to 24:00, including repeated triggers within the same second. Gaps separate the months; narrow screens support horizontal scrolling. Hover over a square to see its date and daily total.

Color and square area share a quartile mapping based on nonzero full-day totals from all recorded dates except Mondays. Q1, the median, Q3 and the maximum define the green, yellow and red scale, shared across all years. Tiny solid squares indicate zero triggers, hollow squares indicate missing logs, and loading failures are marked separately. Totals reflect available logs only.
